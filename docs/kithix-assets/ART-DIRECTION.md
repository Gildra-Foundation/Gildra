# Kith’ix material kit

The raid uses its own charcoal-plum basalt prison seals, large bronze clamps and muted chartreuse inlays. Broad painted planes replace tiny engraving. This differs from Venomous Abyss' jade and cobra ornament and Voidspire's violet crystals.

The scene is a simplified environmental interpretation of the actual raid loading screen, preserving suspended prison monoliths, purple organic cavern, central island and orange pools. It is decorative artwork, not a map or a boss portrait.

Complete visible hub kit: scene with responsive exports, picture frame, nine-slice panel, nine-slice button, root ornament. The overview uses the same scene, panel and button. Navigation reads the artwork from the raid data. No extra mechanics, skill icons or boss portraits are inferred.

Native candidates, exact prompts, QA and proofs: `/home/debian/gildra-kithix-art-20261104/asset-sources/`. Public SOURCE files and manifests record selected native hashes and final geometry. Original alpha is retained; no retouching or upscaling. The 600×900 mobile export avoids enlarging the 941px-high original.

Accent `#b5bd7e`: light-on-base contrast9.68, light-on-background12.41. Its hue is close to Altar of Fangs, but the angular purple prison stone and bronze clamps deliberately use a different material and silhouette.
