# Dungeon map sources

Local, optimized copies used by the Midnight Season 2 route planner. Downloaded 2026-09-11 and resized to the planner canvas.

- Altar of Fangs and Den of Nalorakk: Blizzard in-game atlas captures distributed through Wowhead (`wow.zamimg.com`).
- Murder Row, The Blinding Vale, and Voidscar Arena: Blizzard in-game atlas captures from 4Fansites.
- Kings' Rest and Temple of Sethraliss: annotated Blizzard atlas captures from Raider.IO's published route guides.
- Ruby Life Pools: existing project asset.

The source pages and current dungeon guide links are exposed in each dungeon definition in `components/wow/mythic/dungeonRoutes.ts`.
