# Mythic+ media sources

- Dungeon backdrops are locally optimized versions of the corresponding Wowhead Midnight dungeon-guide header artwork.
- Spell icons are local 64×64 WebP derivatives of the published World of Warcraft catalog media already available through Gildra's catalog API.
- NPC portraits are local 160×160 WebP derivatives of Wowhead model-viewer thumbnails resolved from the matching NPC records.

The application serves these files locally so combat cards and tooltips do not depend on third-party requests at runtime.
