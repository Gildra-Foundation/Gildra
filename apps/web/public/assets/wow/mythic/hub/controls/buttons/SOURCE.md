# Mythic+ themed painted button assets

Created with the built-in imagegen tool, one separate generation for each dungeon. All exteriors retain native alpha, faces are dark/filled and the central rails have no crest. Native copies and complete prompts are archived at /home/debian/gildra-mythic-material-release-20261025/asset-sources/buttons. Only measured transparent-margin trim, proportional768px resize and WebP encoding were applied with Sharp. Existing dungeon frames/art were not modified. Manifest contains native/final dimensions, SHA256, crop and native-pixel nine-slice cuts; text is rendered separately in HTML.

## Ruby Life Pools

Native: /home/debian/.codex/generated_images/01a16876-3f10-79a0-aa1d-4b78bde337fb/exec-0c9bc1f4-e405-4ad0-a544-8e18ab2e2468.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: real raster UI button asset for the Gildra World of Warcraft Mythic+ hub. Create ONE elegant low horizontal Ruby Life Pools button, isolated on a genuinely transparent background. Style: deliberately hand-painted Warcraft game UI, broad simplified brushwork, clean confident silhouette, low to moderate contrast, NO photographic textures, NO grain, NO micro-detail or noisy bevels. Materials and palette: warm antique gold rails over dark slate, small smooth crimson ruby in each end cap, quiet burgundy face; draconic sanctuary elegance. Composition: very wide, low button with its whole silhouette visible and generous transparent margins, roughly 7:1 painted silhouette, centered on a wide 3:1 canvas. Compact distinct end caps must occupy only the outermost 10–12 percent on each side. The central 75 percent must be blank, very dark, nearly opaque and visually quiet to hold readable live HTML text. Its thin straight top and bottom rails must be constant-height uninterrupted lines across the entire middle 75 percent: no central crest, no diamonds, no emblem or rising ornament in the middle, nothing that deforms when stretched by CSS nine-slicing. Keep a large unobstructed center face and thin restrained inner highlights. Rounded or organically chamfered corners. The face is filled opaque dark material; only the exterior is alpha transparent. No text, letters, logo, numbers, watermark, mockup, background scene or multiple buttons.
