# Individual dungeon scenes — 25 October 2026

Built-in image_gen. Backgrounds continue the existing locally served dungeon-guide header artwork in ../backgrounds (provenance in ../SOURCE.md). Each frame edits the approved ../ruby-sanctuary/map-frame.webp geometry, with an actual transparent centre and exterior. Originals retained outside the web bundle. Sharp only encodes, resizes and crops; it does not synthesize or paint imagery. Map pixels, coordinates, IDs, counts and membership are unchanged.

Location identity checked against [Blizzard Season 2](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24294369/), [Coiled Isle / Altar of Fangs](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24293963/follow-the-snakes-to-the-coiled-isle-for-new-adventures) and the corresponding existing dungeon-guide art. Atmosphere is decorative interpretation, not a claim about encounter mechanics.
