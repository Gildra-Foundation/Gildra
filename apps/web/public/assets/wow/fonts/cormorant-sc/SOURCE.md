# Cormorant SC for the large titles

The four `cormorant-sc-600-*.woff2` files are the unmodified Cormorant SC
SemiBold subsets served by Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Cormorant+SC:wght@600`,
fetched 2026-11-11). The family's [metadata](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/cormorantsc/METADATA.pb)
lists Cyrillic, Cyrillic Extended, Latin and Latin Extended.

shape below about 24 px, so card titles and labels stay in the display face.
