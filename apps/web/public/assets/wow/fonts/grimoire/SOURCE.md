# Kurale for the connected character book

`kurale-regular.ttf` is the unmodified Kurale Regular TrueType font from the
official [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/kurale/Kurale-Regular.ttf).
The repository's [metadata](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/kurale/METADATA.pb)
lists Cyrillic, Cyrillic Extended, Latin and Latin Extended. Local `fc-query`
confirmed U+0400–U+045F and additional Cyrillic glyphs in this exact file.
