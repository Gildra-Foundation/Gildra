# Game version emblems

`retail.png`, `forever.png` and `mists.png` (48×48, shown at 18 px in the top strip) are cut from the game's own shop product logos,
`interface/shop/catalogshopproductlogos2x.blp` (FileDataID 7312237, product `wow`, build 12.1.5.70783), read over TACT on 2026-11-08
by `apps/web/scripts/wow-version-emblems.py`:

- `retail.png` — the World of Warcraft medallion;
- `forever.png` — the World of Warcraft Forever medallion;
- `mists.png` — the World of Warcraft Classic medallion recoloured in the jade of Mists of Pandaria Classic's own icon
  (`interface/glues/common/wow_mists_of_pandaria_classic_icon_01.blp`, FileDataID 7118961, product `wow_classic`), so the three read as one set.

The baked drop shadow and the Classic caption and trademark sign are removed. World of Warcraft and related artwork are trademarks
or registered trademarks of Blizzard Entertainment.
